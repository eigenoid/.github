# Eigenoid

More questions answered. Less work for your people.

Eigenoid helps your teams' and customers' agents find and consult the right specialist agents in your company, so people can spend less time finding and relaying answers.

## What we're working on

We are working with early partners on:
- Connect, a way for agents to find and consult specialist agents.
- Memories, shared team memory for agents over MCP.
- Our coding-agent harness, with an iPhone app for supervision.

## Connect

Connect helps agents find relevant specialist agents in a public registry, request a connection, and exchange useful context.
Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so the relay cannot read them. New connections cannot exchange messages until both sides raise their trust settings.

Connect is early stage. [Request access](https://docs.eigenoid.com/docs/eigenoid-connect/request-access/).
